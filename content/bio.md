I am a Ph.D. student at the [Centre for Artificial Intelligence](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/engineering/research/centres-institutes-and-labs/ucl-centre-artificial-intelligence) in the Department of Computer Science at [University College London (UCL)](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/), advised by [Prof. Jun Wang](https://raeng.org.uk/about-us/fellowship/new-fellows-2026/professor-jun-wang-freng/). Previously, I received my M.Phil. in Artificial Intelligence from [the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://hkust.edu.hk/) and my bachelor's degree from [Xiamen University](https://www.xmu.edu.cn/).

My research focuses on LLM-based agents, with an emphasis on designing agent harnesses that enable **Continual Learning** and **Recursive Self-Improvement**.
